Education



In a green city, education isn't just about the traditional classroom setting—it's about creating a holistic learning experience where students engage with the environment around them, fostering the next generation of environmental leaders. Green cities integrate sustainability into both their physical infrastructure and educational programs, creating environments that are not only eco-friendly but also deeply connected to the broader goals of sustainable development.
